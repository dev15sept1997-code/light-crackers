Diwali Cracker Safety Tips

  • 🪣 Keep a bucket of water & sand nearby.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Children only under adult supervision.
  • 👕 Wear cotton clothes; avoid loose synthetics.
  • 🌳 Burst in open areas, away from vehicles & wires.
  • 🚫 Never relight a dud — soak it in water.
  • 🐾 Be kind to pets, elderly & patients.
